Prospect Rank: rankings

Badges on eBay combine both sources below. Either one works on its own.

MLB Pipeline

Loads the Top 100 and every club's Top 30 from MLB.com, then refreshes them weekly.

FanGraphs: The Board

On The Board, use Export Data and load the CSV here. The hitters and pitchers views export separately, so select both files at once, or load one and then add the other. The columns used are Name, Org, Pos, Top 100, Org Rk and FV. Re-export and load again whenever The Board is updated.

Or paste a CSV or a plain list

Ranking history

Builds each player's history from the yearly FanGraphs files, named with the year and ending in r (Report) or u (Updated): 2025r.csv, 2025u.csv. The newest file also becomes the FanGraphs list above. Hover a badge on eBay to see the chart.

Sold prices

While you browse eBay with the Sold items filter on (or open a sold listing), every sold listing that names a ranked player is saved here with its price, date and the player's ranks at the time. Turn this off in the popup. On a Best Offer sale eBay shows only the asking price, so those are marked and left out of medians.

To show them on the Prospect Board dashboard, open its Sources panel and replace the Sold prices file with the downloaded CSV.
